# SOP-02: Commercial Discounting & Quotation Guidelines

**Document Reference**: ENLIGHT-SOP-2026-02  
**Effective Date**: January 15, 2026  
**Target Audience**: Sales Executives, Senior Account Managers, Commercial Desk  
**Visibility Classification**: Salesperson & Above (Commercial Operating Rules)

---

## 1. Quotation Validity Period & Commodity Index Rule

Due to high daily volatility in raw material and steel scrap prices on regional and international commodity exchanges:

1. **Standard Quotation Validity**: All formal sales quotations are valid strictly for **24 hours** from the timestamp of issuance.
2. **Expired Quotes**: If a customer confirms an order after 24 hours, the sales representative must check the current daily rate sheet in Enlight Sales OS before confirming the booking.
3. **Price Lock Commitments**: Verbal price locks are strictly prohibited. A price lock is only valid upon receipt of a written Purchase Order (PO) and a minimum **10% booking advance**.

---

## 2. Tiered Volume Discount Slabs

Sales representatives have discretionary discounting authority based on confirmed single-order shipment tonnage:

| Order Quantity Slab        | Permissible Discount per Metric Ton | Authority Level Required        |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------- | :------------------------------ |
| **5 MT to 25 MT**          | **INR 0 / MT** (Standard List Rate) | Standard Sales Representative   |
| **25 MT to 50 MT**         | Up to **INR 500 / MT** discount     | Standard Sales Representative   |
| **50 MT to 100 MT**        | Up to **INR 1,200 / MT** discount   | Senior Sales Representative     |
| **100 MT to 200 MT**       | Up to **INR 2,000 / MT** discount   | Sales Manager Approval          |
| **> 200 MT (Mega Orders)** | Custom project pricing              | Vice President / Admin Approval |

_Important: Applying a discount that reduces the transaction gross margin below the 4.5% baseline floor requires mandatory managerial override._

---

## 3. Competitor Price Matching Protocol

When a registered recurring customer presents a verified competing quotation from recognized distributors (e.g., Jindal Steels, Essar, or Steel Authority authorized stockists):

1. **Verification**: Obtain a written quotation copy or formal WhatsApp quote stating identical material grade, dimensional tolerances, and delivery terms.
2. **Matching Authority**:
   - Sales reps may match competitor prices up to a maximum price differential of **INR 800 / MT**.
   - If the competitor price differential exceeds INR 800 / MT, escalate to the Sales Manager with the competitor quotation attachment.

---

## 4. Taxation & Regulatory Compliance

- All quoted prices are **exclusive of Goods & Services Tax (GST)** unless explicitly stated as "GST Inclusive".
- Metal commodities attract statutory **18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9% for intra-state, or IGST 18% for inter-state)**.
- Dispatch vehicles cannot exit the stockyard gate without an active government E-Way Bill and digitally signed tax invoice.
