## 2. Primary Mill Sourcing Formulas & Costing Structure

Enlight Metals procures prime material under annual direct volume off-take memorandums of understanding (MOUs) with Tier-1 steel producers:

1. **JSW Steel (Hot Rolled / Cold Rolled / Coated Products)**:
   - Base Price: Monthly Published Platts Index minus **INR 1,450 / MT** MOU volume rebate.
   - Cash Discount: **1.25%** for payment within 7 days of mill dispatch.
2. **Tata Steel (TMT Rebars & Wire Rods)**:
   - Base Price: Quarterly regional billing rate minus **INR 1,100 / MT** distributor discount.
   - Freight Subsidy: Jamshedpur/Kalinganagar to Central Stockyard freight rebate of **INR 650 / MT**.
3. **Jindal Stainless Limited (SS304 / SS316 Cold Rolled Sheets)**:
   - Base Price: Monthly LME Nickel/Chromium indexed formula minus **INR 4,200 / MT** alloy surcharge concession.

## 3. Corporate Target Margin Equation

The internal benchmark for financial viability is calculated via the following proprietary formula:

$$\text{Corporate Target Selling Price} = (\text{Mill Net Landed Cost} + \text{Inward Freight} + \text{Handling}) \times (1 + \text{Corporate Target Margin})$$

Where:

- **Baseline Corporate Target Margin**: Strictly set at **6.2% Corporate Margin** across all carbon steel product lines, and **8.5%** on stainless steel and non-ferrous alloys.
- **Cost of Working Capital Allowance**: Built into commercial modeling at **9.5% per annum**.
- **Buffer for Price Volatility**: Maintained at a minimum contingency reserve of **INR 400 / MT**.

## 4. Year-End Tier-1 Mill Volume Rebates

In addition to transactional margins, Enlight Metals receives backend annual volume performance bonuses upon reaching target procurement thresholds:

- **Level 1 (50,000 MT/year)**: Flat **INR 350 / MT** retrospective rebate on total annual tonnage.
- **Level 2 (100,000 MT/year)**: Flat **INR 750 / MT** retrospective rebate on total annual tonnage.
- **Level 3 (150,000 MT/year+ Elite Partner)**: Flat **INR 1,200 / MT** retrospective rebate + 50% co-marketing fund contribution.
