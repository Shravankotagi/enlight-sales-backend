# SOP-03: Manager Approval Matrix & Credit Policy

**Document Reference**: ENLIGHT-SOP-2026-03  
**Effective Date**: February 1, 2026  
**Target Audience**: Sales Managers, Area Managers, Credit Risk Officers, Directors  
**Visibility Classification**: Managers & Above (Managerial Governance & Risk)

---

## 1. Credit Limit Approval Matrix

To balance aggressive sales growth with working capital protection, credit terms are subject to the following strict approval authority thresholds:

| Authority Tier              | Maximum Credit Line per Customer    | Maximum Credit Days Allowed | Required Security Collateral                                 |
| :-------------------------- | :---------------------------------- | :-------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sales Representative**    | **INR 0** (Advance only)            | 0 Days                      | N/A                                                          |
| **Sales Manager**           | Up to **INR 25,00,000 (25 Lakhs)**  | Up to **30 Days**           | Signed Credit Application + 3 Undated Security Cheques (PDC) |
| **Vice President / Admin**  | Up to **INR 1,00,00,000 (1 Crore)** | Up to **45 Days**           | Audited Financials (2 Years) + Personal Director Guarantee   |
| **Board of Directors / MD** | **> INR 1,00,00,000 (1 Crore+)**    | Up to **60 to 90 Days**     | Irrevocable Bank Guarantee (BG) or Letter of Credit (LC)     |

## 2. Gross Margin Floor & Exception Overrides

1. **Standard Baseline Floor**:
   - The default operational floor margin is **4.5% Gross Margin** on all catalog items.
   - Any quote with a margin between 4.0% and 4.5% flags a warning in the system.
2. **Managerial Margin Exception Authority**:
   - Sales Managers are authorized to approve quotes down to **3.0% Gross Margin** exclusively under the following circumstances:
     - The order volume exceeds **200 Metric Tons (MT)**.
     - The customer account has an exemplary payment score (> 90% on-time payment track record over the past 12 months).
     - Full payment is secured via 100% advance or Letter of Credit (LC).
3. **Sub-3.0% Deals**:
   - Any transaction with a gross margin below 3.0% represents negative net economic value after accounting for cost of capital and logistics overhead. It requires unanimous approval from the Finance Director and Managing Director.

## 3. Account Default & Recovery Protocol

1. **Day 31 to 45 (Grace Period)**: Automated WhatsApp reminders and commercial follow-ups by the assigned sales representative.
2. **Day 46 to 60 (Escalation)**: Formal notice of interest penalty (18% p.a.). Immediate block placed on all new order bookings and pending dispatches across all Enlight stockyards.
3. **Day 61+ (Legal Enforcement)**: File Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act notice utilizing deposited security cheques; initiate formal recovery proceedings.
