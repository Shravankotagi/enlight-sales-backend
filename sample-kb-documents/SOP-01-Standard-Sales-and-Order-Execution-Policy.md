# SOP-01: Standard Sales & Order Execution Policy

**Document Reference**: ENLIGHT-SOP-2026-01  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Target Audience**: All Sales Representatives, Customer Service, and Operations  
**Visibility Classification**: All Employees (Public Sales Operations)

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory guidelines for processing customer inquiries, generating quotations, and executing confirmed metal distribution orders across all Enlight Metals sales channels.

---

## 2. Inquiry Processing & SLA

1. **Inquiry Logging**: All inbound customer requests received via WhatsApp, email, or telephone must be recorded in Enlight Sales OS within 15 minutes of receipt.
2. **Quotation Turnaround Time (SLA)**:
   - Standard catalog products (TMT bars, standard HR/CR sheets): Quotations must be issued to the customer within **4 business hours**.
   - Custom structural fabrication or specialized alloy inquiries: Quotations must be issued within **8 business hours**.

---

## 3. Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

To ensure freight efficiency and warehouse handling viability, the following Minimum Order Quantities are strictly enforced:

- **TMT Rebars (Fe500D / Fe550D)**: Minimum **5 Metric Tons (MT)**.
- **Hot Rolled (HR) & Cold Rolled (CR) Coils/Sheets**: Minimum **10 Metric Tons (MT)** per standard coil bundle.
- **Structural Steel (Beams, Channels, Angles)**: Minimum **3 Metric Tons (MT)**.
- **Stainless Steel (SS304 / SS316 Sheets & Pipes)**: Minimum **1 Metric Ton (MT)**.
- **Aluminum & Non-Ferrous Extrusions**: Minimum **500 Kilograms (KG)**.

_Note: Any order below the MOQ must be approved as a retail split-bundle order with a flat INR 1,500 handling surcharge._

---

## 4. Standard Payment Terms

1. **New Customer Accounts**:
   - First 3 orders require **100% Advance Payment** via RTGS/NEFT prior to vehicle loading and dispatch.
2. **Recurring Customer Accounts (Credit-Approved)**:
   - Standard credit terms: **Net 30 Days** from the date of the tax invoice.
   - Payments delayed beyond 30 days incur a commercial interest charge of **1.5% per month (18% per annum)** calculated daily.
   - Customers with unpaid overdue invoices exceeding 45 days will be placed on automatic dispatch hold.

---

## 5. Logistics & Freight Policies

1. **Ex-Works (Factory/Warehouse Gate)**:
   - The buyer is responsible for arranging transport, transit insurance, and freight payment.
   - Enlight Metals provides complimentary vehicle loading at our stockyards during standard operating hours (08:00 to 18:00).
2. **FOR Destination (Free On Road Delivery)**:
   - Enlight Metals arranges dedicated freight delivery to the customer's specified jobsite or plant.
   - Freight charges are quoted separately and added to the commercial invoice based on distance and vehicle tonnage.
3. **Demurrage Policy**:
   - Customers receive **48 hours of free unloading time** upon vehicle arrival at the destination.
   - Detention/demurrage charges of **INR 2,500 per day per vehicle** apply for unloading delays exceeding 48 hours.

---

## 6. Inspection & Quality Claims

- Material test certificates (MTC) according to IS 1786 and ASTM standards accompany every dispatch.
- Physical quantity or dimensional discrepancy claims must be reported within **72 hours** of material receipt with stamped weighbridge slips and photographic evidence.
